Feta Pasta

Description

Feta Pasta is a classic dish with a mediterranean flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 Box Penne Pasta
  • Olive Oil
  • Crumbled Feta Cheese
  • Cherry Tomatoes
  • Basil Leaves
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cook pasta in boiling water for 11 minutes, add salt to taste, cooking until tender
  2. When the pasta is halfway done, cook tomatoes in frying pan on low heat until they start to burst
  3. Add feta cheese to tomatos and cook for another minute
  4. Drain the pasta, taste to ensure cooked through
  5. Combine tomatoes and cheese with pasta and add basil
  6. Serve hot and fresh!
