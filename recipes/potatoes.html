Roasted Potatoes

Description

Roasted potatos are a classic side piece that go with any dish. The process is fairly simple and involves light prep work and roasting in the oven.

Ingredients

  • 3 Russet Potatoes
  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic Powder
  • Ground Basil or Parsley
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees
  2. Chop potatoes into small cubes or disks
  3. Lay out potatoes on a baking sheet and douse with olive oil
  4. Season with garlic powder, basil, and salt
  5. Bake for 25 minutes, or until golden brown
