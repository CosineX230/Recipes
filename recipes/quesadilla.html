Quesadilla

Description

Quesadillas are a Mexican classic combining a crispy tortilla exterior with melted cheese inside.

Ingredients

  • 1 Tortilla
  • Cheese
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a frying pan or griddle
  2. Spread cheese inside of tortilla and fold in half. Cut into 2-3 wedges
  3. Fry up a few wedges at a time on a griddle (or 1 in a frying pan)
  4. Flip and fry other side. Serve when cheese is melted and outside is golden brown
